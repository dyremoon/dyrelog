About Dyrelog

Dyrelog started out as a project to get a sleek and lightweight dps meter in EQL for myself and a group of friends. I found other options, and they're fantastic as well, but I get overwhelmed when there's too much data and information at my disposal, so I wanted something that did just dps, and did it really well.

I'm a software engineer and enjoy building projects, and I've been playing MMOs my whole life. I took inspiration from dps meters from other games so that Dyrelog feels familiar, and added a fun leaderboard for us all to track our dps on if we so choose.

This is a fan project, built and maintained by one person in their spare time, not an official tool. If you have any feedback or bug reports, please submit them below.

What it looks like

Five ways to see your dps, from a full bar list down to a tiny corner badge, plus a deep-dive analysis tool and a leaderboard to compare against everyone else.

Bars

The default live meter, familiar and sleek: a ranked bar for every combatant in the fight, dps and total damage front and center while you play.

Mini mode & Circle

Two small, out-of-the-way styles for when Bars is more than you need. Mini mode is a simple, low-weight readout you can tuck to the side of the screen without it fighting for your attention; Circle is a different, fun take, a small round badge that's easy to drop in a corner and forget about until you glance down at it.

Combat Analysis

The deep dive: dps over time, a full combatant table, per-ability breakdowns, procs, even an incoming-damage view, all built from the same log the live meter already reads, no separate export or upload step. It's the depth you'd expect from a standalone parser, without ever leaving the overlay.
