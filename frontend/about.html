About Dyrelog

Dyrelog started out as a project to get a sleek and lightweight dps meter in EQL for myself and a group of friends. I found other options, and they're fantastic as well, but I get overwhelmed when there's too much data and information at my disposal, so I wanted something that did just dps, and did it really well.

I'm a software engineer and enjoy building projects, and I've been playing MMOs my whole life. I took inspiration from dps meters from other games so that Dyrelog feels familiar, and added a fun leaderboard for us all to track our dps on if we so choose.

This is a fan project, built and maintained by one person in their spare time, not an official tool. If you have any feedback or bug reports, please submit them below.

What it looks like

The overlay has two display styles — a full bar list, or a small circular badge — plus a separate Combat Analysis window for a deeper breakdown of any fight.

Bars — the default live dps meter
Circle — a small always-on-top badge
Combat Analysis — the deeper breakdown
