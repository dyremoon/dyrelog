Layout

Leaves just each row's name and dps on screen, header, icons, the timer and rank numbers fade out until you move your mouse back over the overlay.

Circle replaces the whole overlay with a small round badge showing just your dps and the fight timer, Mini mode doesn't apply while it's on. Click the badge, right-click it, or use its gear button any time to jump to a menu with Analysis, Leaderboards, this Settings window, and Switch to Bars.

Only matters in Circle display style above, resizes the round badge (and the window around it) up or down.

Color

Also recolors this Settings window's own accent (and the Leaderboards/Analysis windows), so the whole app matches the theme you pick here, not just the in-game overlay.

Border is the lightweight outline around each row, it no longer fades out along with Background opacity above, so there's still visible structure between rows even at low opacity.

Separate from "Text" above, that's every row's main text; these recolor the fight timer/status/rank numbers, your own or your pet's name, each row's own "DPS" number (bars and mini mode), and the single total-damage figure (mob-line and mini mode) — wherever each shows up.

Only used by Circle display style (Layout above) — Bars/Mini-bar keep their own separate colors above, this is what actually recolors the round badge itself.

About Dyrelog

Dyrelog started out as a project to get a sleek and lightweight dps meter in EQL for myself and a group of friends. I found other options, and they're fantastic as well, but I get overwhelmed when there's too much data and information at my disposal, so I wanted something that did just dps, and did it really well.

I'm a software engineer and enjoy building projects, and I've been playing MMOs my whole life. I took inspiration from dps meters from other games so that Dyrelog feels familiar, and added a fun leaderboard for us all to track our dps on if we so choose.

This is a fan project, built and maintained by one person in their spare time, not an official tool. If you have any feedback or bug reports, please submit them below.
