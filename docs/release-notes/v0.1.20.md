A reliability release: your kills are much harder to lose, uploads are smaller, and difficulty now says D0.

**What's new**
- Kills are no longer lost when something goes wrong. If Dyrelog can't reach its server, the server is busy, or you close the app mid-upload, the kill is saved on your PC and sent automatically later. The popup tells you when this happens.
- If your Discord login expires during an upload, the kill waits for you to log back in instead of being dropped.
- Dyrelog now uploads only combat lines (damage, heals, deaths and spell casts). Chat, tells and loot never leave your PC.
- During boss fights, Dyrelog uploads every 5 seconds instead of every second. Kills are verified the same way.
- Kills without a difficulty are now called **D0** everywhere, matching what the community uses.

**Fixes and polish**
- Submitting the same kill twice now just says it was already submitted.
- If a fight's log is too large to upload, the popup explains why instead of skipping it quietly.
- A very large log file that reappears is read in pieces, so the meter stays responsive.
- On the website, a cancelled or failed Discord login explains what happened, and the leaderboards show a clear message if they're temporarily unavailable.

### How to install and update

**Already have Dyrelog 0.1.17 or newer?** You don't need to download anything. Dyrelog shows an update banner at the top of the meter within about an hour (or right away when you start it). Click it and Dyrelog downloads the update, installs it and restarts. You can also check any time in **Settings → What's New → Check for updates**.

**New install, or on 0.1.16 or older?**
1. Download **Dyrelog-Setup-0.1.20.exe** below. (Ignore the `.blockmap` and `latest.yml` files; those are for the updater.)
2. Run it. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Open Dyrelog and pick your EverQuest folder. In game, type `/log on` if you haven't before.

Windows 10 or 11 only.
