A polish release: clearer wording everywhere, a safer login, and a few fixes.

**What's new**
- In Ask mode, kills now wait in line. If a second boss dies before you answer the first popup, both are kept and offered one after the other.
- Kills that finish while you're logged out are all kept and offered after you log in, not just the last one.
- Your Discord login is now stored encrypted by Windows. You stay logged in through the update.

**Fixes and polish**
- One set of names across the app and website: Bars, Mini and Circle; My Kills; Base for kills without a difficulty (no more "D0").
- Shorter, plainer text in Settings, the popup, the leaderboard window and error messages.
- The usage statistics question now defaults to "No thanks", so pressing Enter doesn't opt you in.
- Sound names in Settings are easier to read, and the installer no longer carries duplicate sound files.
- The update banner's tooltip now says what it does.

### How to install and update

**Already have Dyrelog 0.1.17 or newer?** You don't need to download anything. Dyrelog shows an update banner at the top of the meter within about an hour (or right away when you start it). Click it and Dyrelog downloads the update, installs it and restarts. You can also check any time in **Settings → What's New → Check for updates**.

**New install, or on 0.1.16 or older?**
1. Download **Dyrelog-Setup-0.1.18.exe** below. (Ignore the `.blockmap` and `latest.yml` files; those are for the updater.)
2. Run it. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Open Dyrelog and pick your EverQuest folder. In game, type `/log on` if you haven't before.

Windows 10 or 11 only.
