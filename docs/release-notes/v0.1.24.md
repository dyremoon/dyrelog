### How to install and update

**Already have Dyrelog 0.1.17 or newer?** You don't need to download anything. Dyrelog shows an update banner at the top of the meter within about an hour (or right away when you start it). Click it to install, or check any time in **Settings → What's New → Check for updates**.

**New install?**
1. Download **Dyrelog-Setup-0.1.24.exe** below. (Ignore the `.blockmap` and `latest.yml` files; those are for the updater.)
2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. That warning is normal for small apps without a paid code-signing certificate.
3. Open Dyrelog and pick your EverQuest folder. In game, type `/log on` if you haven't before.

Windows 10 or 11 only.
