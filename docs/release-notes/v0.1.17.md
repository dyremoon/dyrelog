If you're coming from 0.1.7, this is a big one. Everything since then is below.

**New: submit kills to the leaderboard**
- Log in with Discord inside the app (Settings → Options → Account).
- Choose Ask, Auto or Off for submitting boss kills. A small popup shows what happened with each kill.
- Boss fights upload as you play, and the kill is checked when the boss dies.
- Your submitted kills and their review status show in Leaderboards → My Kills and on your profile page on the website.

**Meter**
- Mini mode redesign with its own saved size, total damage line, and pet row.
- Circle mode: drag to move, switch fights from its menu.
- Separate sliders for text, icons and timer size. Text size no longer zooms the whole window.
- Icons to switch between Bars, Mini and Circle, with hover tooltips.
- Boss difficulty (D1 to D4) shown next to the boss.
- Fixed windows slowly growing while you drag them on some display scaling settings.

**Combat Analysis**
- Fixed the fight breakdown not showing.
- Hover the DPS graph for exact values. More detail on procs.

**Parsing**
- Named pets (like a necromancer's pet) are now credited to you when the log shows it attacking on your command.
- Better handling of DoTs and spell damage, and of groupmates who were being mistaken for mobs.

**Updates**
- The app checks for updates about once an hour while it's open, not every 6 hours.
- Click the update banner to download, install and restart. Closing to tray or the exit prompt no longer blocks the install.
- Settings → What's New shows recent release notes.

**Fixes and safety**
- Only one copy of Dyrelog can run at a time, so a kill can't be sent twice. Opening it again brings up the running copy.
- If your log file doesn't exist yet, Dyrelog waits for it ("type /log on") instead of doing nothing.
- Settings, history and login are saved in a way that survives a crash or power cut.
- Links only open trusted sites, and app windows can't be sent to outside web pages.
- Clearer error messages when the server or your internet is down.

**Privacy**
- Optional usage statistics, off unless you choose Allow. Details: https://dyrelog.pages.dev/privacy.html

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
